Coaching priorities in the reports

The August review highlighted a step-across tendency during tee swings, a body-and-arms-together pattern, and early barrel wrap in some swings.

Training priorities:

  • Stride-and-hold to check landing direction.
  • Stay in the back hip during the forward move.
  • Top-hand and one-hand connection drills.
  • Keep the bat traveling through the hitting zone longer.

September interpretation: The report noted cleaner stride direction, somewhat improved separation, longer through-zone action, and a more controlled finish. It also documented persistent limitations.

A second contact-quality score was deliberately not assigned because the September footage did not consistently show enough early ball flight.

How the coaching loop worked

01 • Record

Save the original side/back, tee/toss videos.

02 • Review

Record observable patterns and one or two training goals.

03 • Practice

Use targeted drills between sessions.

04 • Retest

Record again and compare with explicit uncertainty.

What this case does not establish

  • No proven causal effect of the training drills.
  • No validated 3D biomechanics or joint-force measurement.
  • No automated score generated by the current public Python code.
  • No measured increase in exit velocity or a valid September contact-quality score.
  • No claim that this single player represents all program participants.
