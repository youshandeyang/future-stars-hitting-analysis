# Future Stars family swing checklist

A phone, safe practice space and an adult are enough. No coding, special software or pitching machine needed.

**Record the Swing → Watch & Compare → Pick One Thing → Practice a Change → Record Again → See What Changed**

## Set up safely

- [ ] **Adult checks first.** Clear the practice area. Use suitable bat/balls, a batting helmet and safe net/backstop. Keep others clear. Stop for pain or fatigue.

- [ ] **Choose tee or adult soft/front toss.** A tee is the simplest start. Toss only with safe adult positioning and soft training balls in suitable space; hard-ball toss needs a proper protective screen.

- [ ] **Phase I minimum: one fixed side view.** Aim roughly square to the hitter. Keep phone, tripod and operator outside bat and ball paths, behind protection when exposed. Never film unprotected directly in front of a live hitter.

- [ ] **Frame the action.** Include the whole body, full bat swing and plate/ball if possible. Use good light, a stable phone and no zoom changes. Make a short test clip before starting.

- [ ] **Extra angles are optional.** Front/rear views require safe protected positions. Phase II can add Side Tee, Back Tee, Side Toss and Back Toss. These are separate takes, not synchronized 3D evidence.

## Follow the six step loop

1. **Record the Swing.** Record 8-10 comfortable swings as the baseline, including ordinary attempts. This is a practical suggestion, not a validated test.

2. **Watch & Compare.** Watch normal speed, then slow motion. Compare the player with their own earlier clips. Note only clearly visible moments.

3. **Pick One Thing.** With a parent/coach, choose one repeated, visible pattern and one low-risk cue, such as a comfortable balanced finish.

4. **Practice a Change.** Try a few relaxed repetitions with one cue. Keep it enjoyable; never force a position or push through discomfort.

5. **Record Again.** Record 8-10 swings using the same drill, tee height or toss setup, bat/ball, handedness, camera angle, height, distance, zoom and frame rate.

6. **See What Changed.** Compare several swings. Mark improved, unchanged, worsened or unclear; save before/after timestamps. A change in one session does not prove the cue caused it.

## Use AI only if you want to

**Privacy first.** Keep clips local by default. An adult checks the chosen service's age rules, consent requirements, privacy, retention and training settings. Do not upload identifiable minors without guardian authorization and permission under the service's rules. Remove names, faces, voices, location and team/school clues where possible; cropping alone does not guarantee anonymity. Keep youth media out of the public repository.

**Keep a person in charge.** Use supported files; a parent/coach reviews each suggestion. AI can be wrong. Never grade hidden/blurred events, diagnose, or treat adult MLB swings as youth standards.

### Optional copy and paste prompt

Compare baseline/retest clips of the same drill and view. Cite visible evidence and timestamps. Separate observations, interpretations and uncertainty. For hidden, off-screen or blurred events, say “not enough information.” Suggest one low-risk cue for parent/coach review. Never invent angles, speed, force, diagnoses or 3D motion. Keep separate takes separate. Do not use adult MLB players as youth norms.
